# Proactive notification templates — deterministic, never sent to an LLM (amounts must not come from a model).
# Owner: Member 1 · PROACTIVE_FEATURE_CHANGES.md §4.4
# Placeholders (filled from the LEDGER / config): {amount} {settlement_id} {cause_text} {kyc_link}
# One block per [lang.variant]. Missing language -> en-IN. Lines starting with # are comments.
#   retriggerable  payout can be re-sent; waiting for the merchant's yes (Haan / Abhi nahi buttons)
#   over_limit     above the auto-limit: a team member verifies (step-up)
#   bank_details   IFSC wrong and not fixed yet: no money step
#   kyc            KYC pending: never retriggered
#   declined       merchant said "abhi nahi" to the payout
#   generic        anything else (the settlement could not be actioned automatically)
